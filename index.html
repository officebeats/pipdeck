PipDeck: Multi-Platform Hardware Companion

An open-source desktop companion display engineered for Kindle KOReader & ZenOS E-Ink (Primary Tier 1) and ESP32 Micro-Displays (Secondary Tier 2) with 1-click non-technical install, 12h time, and instant Light/Dark mode.

Zero-Hassle Installation for Non-Technical Kindle Users

Choose the simplest method that fits your workflow. No terminal commands or complex file hunting required:

🔌 Method 1: 1-Click Web Drive Installer

Plug your Kindle Paperwhite into your computer via USB, click below, and pick your Kindle drive. Your browser will automatically copy both the KOReader plugin and KUAL extension!

⚠️ Prerequisite: You must have a jailbroken Kindle with KOReader installed!
Don't know what that is? 👉 Let Me Google That For You 🖱️

🌌 Method 2: ZenPM Store (Zero Cables / Wi-Fi)

If you run ZenOS on your Kindle, open ZenPM (Package Manager) on your device, search for "PipDeck", and tap [Install] over Wi-Fi.

ZenOS > ZenPM > Search "PipDeck" > Install

📦 Method 3: 1-Step Drag & Drop ZIP

Download the pre-structured ZIP package and drag its contents directly to the root of your Kindle drive (e.g. E:\ or /Volumes/Kindle).
